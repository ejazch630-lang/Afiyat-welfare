Our inspiration

Afiyat Welfare is led by CEO Sawera Ejaz. Our main goal is to help women become independent, confident and self-reliant, so that whole families grow stronger.

We look to the lives of Hazrat Khadijah and Hazrat Fatimah (may Allah be pleased with them) and try to walk in their footsteps through our actions, not only our words.

Our mission: reach poor families with education, health and winter support, with honesty and dignity.

Hazrat Khadijah (RA)
A successful businesswoman, known for honest trade and generosity to her community. She inspires our work on women’s independence.
Hazrat Fatimah (RA)
A model of faith, patience and kindness to the poor. She inspires our service to needy families.

Our work

Three focus areas, one goal: lift families up.

📚

Education

Helping children stay in school and learn.

  • School fees
  • Books and stationery
  • Uniforms and bags
  • Girls’ education
🩺

Health

Care that poor families can reach.

  • Free medical camps
  • Medicines
  • Women’s and children’s health
🧣

Winter support

Warmth for the coldest months.

  • Blankets and quilts
  • Warm clothes and shoes
  • Winter packs
🌸

Women empowerment

A hand-up, not a hand-out.

  • Skills training
  • Small business support for widows and single mothers
  • Confidence and guidance
